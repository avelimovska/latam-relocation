Privacy policy

A short note on what data this site collects and why. Updated 25 October 2026.

Who we are

move·uruguay is a personal information project about moving to Uruguay. The site sells no services and takes no payments.

What is collected

The site has no forms, accounts or comments, so we never ask you to enter personal data. For visitor statistics we use Google Analytics: it sets cookies and collects anonymised visit data (pages opened, device type, country, approximate time). Google truncates the IP address to the region level.

Why

To understand which sections are read and in which languages, and to improve the site. Data is not sold or shared with third parties other than Google as the analytics operator.

How to opt out

Block cookies in your browser settings or install the Google Analytics Opt-out extension. The site will work as before.

Your rights

Under Uruguay's Law No. 18.331 on personal data protection you may request access to your data, its correction or deletion. A contact for requests will appear together with the launch of the Telegram channel.
