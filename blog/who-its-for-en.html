Who moving to Uruguay is for

This path won't suit everyone, and that's normal. There are two main groups of people for whom this option is especially suitable.

2 groups
of applicants
Contents01 · Remote workers with a safety cushion02 · Specialists in practical professions03 · What's next
Option 1 · applicant4 points

Remote workers with a safety cushion

  • ✓You have a stable remote income for your family
  • ✓You have already built a financial safety cushion
  • ✓You want to live in a clear jurisdiction, not “jump between visas”
  • ✓A calm pace of life and long-term planning appeal to you
Option 2 · applicant4 points

Specialists in practical professions

  • ✓You have a real, in-demand profession (auto mechanic, beauty salon specialist, electrician, etc.)
  • ✓You are ready to learn Spanish intensively and gradually integrate into the local environment
  • ✓You have financial reserves for the adaptation period
  • ✓You see relocation as a long-term life change, not an experiment
