Privacy policy

How move·uruguay collects, uses and protects visitor data. In short: we do not collect personal data and only use anonymous Google Analytics statistics.

01 · General provisions

General provisions

This policy explains how the move·uruguay website collects, uses and protects information about its visitors. By using the site, you agree to the terms below. Data is handled in line with Uruguay’s Law No. 18.331 on personal data protection.

02 · What data we collect

What data we collect

The site requires no registration and never asks for your name, phone number or documents. We only receive anonymous technical data: device and browser type, approximate location based on IP address, the pages you opened and time spent on the site.

03 · Google Analytics and cookies

Google Analytics and cookies

We use Google Analytics for visitor statistics. The service stores cookies in your browser and sends anonymous data to Google. You can disable cookies in your browser settings or install the official Google Analytics Opt-out add-on — the site will keep working.

04 · Why we use the data

Why we use the data

Only to understand which materials are useful to readers and to improve the site: finish articles, fix mistakes and simplify navigation. We do not use the data for advertising and do not build visitor profiles.

05 · Sharing with third parties

Sharing with third parties

We do not sell or share your data with third parties, except Google as part of Google Analytics. Links to Telegram and other external services lead to third-party sites with their own privacy rules.

06 · Storage and security

Storage and security

Statistics are stored by Google for the period set in the Google Analytics settings. The site runs over the secure HTTPS protocol.

07 · Your rights

Your rights

You can ask what data about you is processed, request that it be corrected or deleted, and opt out of analytics by disabling cookies.

08 · Changes and contacts

Changes and contacts

We may update this policy — the current version is always published on this page with its revision date. For questions about your data, write to us via the “Contacts” section.
