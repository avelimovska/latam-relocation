Page 02 · Requirements

3 basic conditions and a document package

Uruguay's immigration policy is simple: “Can you support yourself and not be a problem for my citizens? Then here's your residency.”

Condition 01✓ required

No criminal record

A clean criminal background is the main requirement for obtaining permanent residency

Condition 02✓ required

Sufficient income

The law requires proof of income sufficient to live on (a job, pension or rental income). In practice, the benchmark is about $800 per person per month

Condition 03✓ required

Legal source of funds

Ability to document the origin of your money

Plus a standard document package: apostilled criminal record certificates from every country you lived in during the last 5 years, birth and marriage certificates, and a vaccination card.

This is not “Squid Game” or a lottery

The process is clear and transparent, and the requirements are known in advance. Citizenship is decided by the Electoral Court (Corte Electoral), and the whole path usually takes 4–7 years from the move.

Your money goes into concrete procedural steps and clear statuses, not into promises.

Page 06 · Questions

Frequently asked questions

How long does it take to obtain temporary and permanent residency in Uruguay?

Processing times depend on the city and the workload of immigration authorities. Temporary residency is granted after submitting the documents. The law sets no deadline for permanent residency decisions; in our experience it takes 6–12 months after submitting the full package.

What level of income is required to apply for residency?

The law does not name an exact amount: you must prove income sufficient to live on. In practice, the benchmark is about $800–$1,000 per person per month; requirements depend on your situation and the city where you apply.

Is it necessary to know Spanish to submit the documents?

Knowledge of Spanish is not mandatory for submitting your application. However, basic Spanish greatly simplifies adaptation and communication in Uruguay.

Is it mandatory to live permanently in Uruguay?

To maintain residency, it is important to keep ties with the country and comply with immigration requirements. Demonstrating actual residence and integration into local life is essential.

In what cases can permanent residency in Uruguay be denied?

A denial is possible in the case of serious criminal convictions (offenses punishable by more than two years in prison) or a repeated criminal history.

How long must you live in Uruguay to obtain a passport?

Married couples and families with children can typically apply for citizenship after about 3 years of legal residence; single applicants usually after about 5 years. The application is then reviewed by the Electoral Court (Corte Electoral), where the queue alone can take up to a year.

In what cases can permanent residency in Uruguay be revoked?

Permanent residency may be revoked if false information or forged documents are discovered, in cases of serious intentional crimes, major violations of immigration law, threats to public safety, or prolonged absence from the country.
